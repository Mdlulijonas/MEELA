About Meela TDI

Meela Training and Development Institute (MTDI) was founded on the visionary insight of its director, Mr. CK Meela.

His unique professional journey—spanning roles as a skills development consultant for various SETA-funded projects and as an educator in both government and private schools—revealed a critical gap in the training landscape.

Mr. Meela identified a persistent disconnect between theoretical knowledge and practical, workplace-ready skills. He observed that students often graduated with qualifications but lacked the hands-on competence and confidence to excel in their chosen careers.

Driven by this need, he established MTDI with a core mission: to champion a practical, hands-on learning methodology that moves beyond theory. The institute is built on the philosophy that true learning happens through application.

To this end, MTDI actively strives to bridge the gap between the classroom and the workplace. A key component of its programmes is facilitating in-service training for learners, ensuring they gain vital work experience, build professional networks, and develop the practical proficiency needed to not only cope but to thrive and lead in their respective fields.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Matric rewrite program?

A Matric rewrite program helps students improve their National Senior Certificate results to unlock better opportunities for tertiary education and employment. Our program offers expert tutors, flexible study options, and focused revision on key subject areas.

How long does the Business Management course take?

The Business Management program typically takes 12-18 months to complete, depending on the specific qualification and study pace.

Is Meela TDI accredited?

Yes, Meela Training and Development Institute is fully accredited with various SETAs and the QCTO, ensuring our programs meet national education standards.

What funding options are available?

We offer various funding options including NSFAS funding, Skills Development Levy, payment plans, and bursaries to make education accessible to all students.

Can I study part-time while working?

Yes, we offer flexible study options including part-time, weekend, and evening classes to accommodate working professionals.

How do I apply for a course?

You can apply online through our application form, visit our campus, or contact us via WhatsApp for assistance with the application process.
